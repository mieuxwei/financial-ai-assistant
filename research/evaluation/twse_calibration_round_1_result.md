# TWSE Calibration Round 1 Result

Date: 2026-08-26  
Decision: **RETAIN AS AI STABILITY DIAGNOSTIC — never use as semantic gold truth**

## Scope and provenance

- 60 public TWSE announcement records covering 60 tickers were evaluated.
- Reviewer A was generated by Gemini 3.1 Pro, as disclosed by the project owner after the first
  analysis run. Its exact prompt, temperature and model revision were not captured in the workbook.
- Reviewer B was independently labelled by Codex before Reviewer A labels were opened.
- Both reviewers are AI systems. This round is therefore an AI-to-AI protocol diagnostic only, not
  human annotation evidence, not a two-human gold set and not approved training data.
- No future return, later news or analyst reaction was used for either label comparison.
- The private agreement artifact contains candidate hashes/IDs and aggregate metrics, but no raw
  announcement text.

## Integrity and QC

- Both XLSX files passed ZIP/XLSX integrity checks with no damaged members.
- Both workbooks contained the same 60 candidate IDs and all required annotation fields were
  complete under direct field validation.
- Reviewer A's exported `qc_status` cells remained static `INCOMPLETE` values from the template.
  They were treated as stale display values; actual required fields and conditional reasons were
  validated directly. Reviewer A was not modified.
- Reviewer B contained 60 `READY` rows, no missing conditional reasons and no spreadsheet formula
  errors.

## Agreement

| Dimension | Raw agreement | Cohen's kappa | Conflicts | Gate |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Event type | 0.650000 | 0.533679 | 21 | Fail |
| Impact | 0.766667 | 0.640411 | 14 | Pass |

Under the historical annotation gate, both kappas had to be at least 0.60, so the generated report
records `PAUSE_AND_REVISE_GUIDELINE`. The project subsequently adopted a zero-human-annotation
route. These kappas are now model-stability diagnostics and are not an adoption gate for semantic
accuracy.

## Dominant disagreement patterns

Event-type disagreements were concentrated rather than random:

- 15 records: Reviewer A `OTHER` vs Reviewer B `MATERIAL_TRANSACTION`.
- 2 records: `OTHER` vs `MANAGEMENT_CHANGE`.
- 1 each: `OTHER` vs `CAPITAL_INCREASE`, `BUYBACK` vs `CAPITAL_REDUCTION`, `OTHER` vs
  `CAPITAL_REDUCTION`, and `OTHER` vs `GUIDANCE`.

Impact disagreements were also concentrated:

- 12 records: Reviewer A `NEUTRAL` vs Reviewer B `AMBIGUOUS`.
- 1 record: `POSITIVE` vs `NEUTRAL`.
- 1 record: `NEGATIVE` vs `AMBIGUOUS`.

The main issue is therefore taxonomy granularity and the boundary between routine/directionless
`NEUTRAL` and genuinely unresolved `AMBIGUOUS`, not broad disagreement on clearly positive or
negative announcements.

## Required next action

1. Preserve the original A/B outputs and disagreement record without human adjudication.
2. Convert agreement, confidence and abstention into versioned diagnostic metadata.
3. Implement frozen text representations and AI-derived proxies as separate feature groups.
4. Evaluate each group only through chronological downstream prediction and walk-forward ablation.
5. Never describe this round as semantic validation, expert review or gold training data.
